IRON MAN

Tony Stark known as Iron Man. He is a genius billionaire, playboy, philanthropist, and the owner of Stark Industries. He is a founding member of the Avengers and has appeared in several Marvel Cinematic Universe films.

THOR

Thor Odinson, also known as the God of Thunder, is a fictional character appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics. He is the Asgardian god of thunder and the wielder of the enchanted hammer Mjolnir.
